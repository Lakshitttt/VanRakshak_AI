What It Does

Three capabilities, one reading of the land

Each capability builds on the last — from raw orbital imagery to a classification the platform can act on and monitor over time.

Satellite Intelligence

Satellite imagery gives the platform a consistent, wide-area view of the landscape for on-demand analysis.

AI Classification

A trained model reads each image and assigns a land-cover class — forest, water, cropland, or urban — with a measured confidence score.

Environmental Monitoring

Readings accumulate into a record the platform can track over time, surfacing forest loss, water change, and urban expansion as they happen.

How It Works

From orbit to a finished report

Every analysis follows the same disciplined sequence.

  1. Satellite

    Imagery is captured or supplied for a given area of interest.

  2. AI

    The ResNet50-based model classifies the land cover shown in the image.

  3. Analysis

    The result is scored for confidence and prepared for review.

  4. Report

    Findings are structured into a clear, shareable summary.

What's Next

Built to grow, one capability at a time

Future development will focus on expanding environmental monitoring, improving historical analysis, and adding more advanced change-detection capabilities.
